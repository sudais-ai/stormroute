STORMROUTE

Weather-Aware Truck Routing — Architecture & Risk-Engine Case Study
PythonDjangoDjango REST FrameworkReact 18TypeScriptViteTailwind CSSMapbox GLVercel
Enter an origin, destination and departure time — StormRoute samples 45+ weather checkpoints along every candidate route and ranks each route with a transparent 0–100 per-mile risk score, so the safest driving option is visible before the wheels move.
